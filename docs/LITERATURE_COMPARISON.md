## A1 - PARTIAL

**Claim.** A1 — Geometry/commensuration: exactly-commensurate asymmetric TTG (theta12 != theta23) with generator-consistent per-layer biaxial strain and machine-precision supercell-BZ alignment, enabling point-by-point full-path comparison with atomistic supercell calculations.

**Reasoning.** The geometric construction is preempted several times over. TTTG-0163 (Nakatsuji-Kawakami-Koshino 2023) builds exactly commensurate asymmetric TTG supercells from an integer generator (m,n,p,q) with closed-form theta12, theta23, and explicitly folds the layer and moire BZs onto the supercell BZ — the user's "folding class" bookkeeping, published. TTTG-0098 (Yang/May-Mann/Zhu/Devakul 2023) enforces exact commensuration for theta12/theta23 = p/q by a per-layer biaxial dilation+twist of the middle layer, explicitly as a device to build an exact periodic approximant, and states K1/K2/K3 then fold onto Gamma, K or M "depending on the exact structure." TTTG-0032 (Xia et al. 2025) repeats this with an analytic identity p*A12' = q*A23'. TTTG-0021 (Devakul et al. 2023) already used a biaxial kappa = cos theta rescaling for the same purpose; TTTG-0043 and TTTG-0192 (Jung group) use per-layer uniform strain as a routine commensuration closer; TTTG-0167 gives the asymmetric p/q catalogue. So "strain-to-commensurate asymmetric TTG with exact high-symmetry folding" is not novel, and the strain here is the generator's own artifact, so including it is consistency, not insight.

What no digest paper does: place the continuum model on the *same* commensurate cell as an atomistic calculation of the *same* structure so that model and TB k-points coincide identically, permitting an interpolation-free per-k comparison. TTTG-0270 does TB-vs-continuum on commensurate TTG but at theta12 = 0; TTTG-0043 benchmarks against atomistic TB but only qualitatively, equal-angle, with acknowledged residual strain and off-nominal twists. The exactness is mostly enabling bookkeeping — but not merely so: the certificate discriminates a wrong angle convention (4e-2 vs 1e-13) and exposes the legacy pairing erratum (spurious 14.35 meV gap at supercell K). Against that, TTTG-0066 shows +-0.02 deg twist disorder washes out spectral features, so machine precision is a numerical-validation property, not physics.

**Preempting papers.** TTTG-0163 — exactly commensurate asymmetric TTG from integer generator (m,n,p,q), analytic angles, explicit layer/moire-to-supercell BZ folding; the strongest preemption. TTTG-0098 — exact commensuration of asymmetric TTG by per-layer biaxial dilation+twist, plus the Gamma/K/M folding-class statement. TTTG-0032 — exact commensurate asymmetric BM with middle-layer deformation and analytic commensuration identity. TTTG-0021 — biaxial kappa = cos theta rescaling as the strain-to-commensurate device, with collinear K1,K2,K3 folding. TTTG-0043 and TTTG-0192 — per-layer uniform strain as a standard commensuration closer in multilayer supercell construction (0043 also has an atomistic TB benchmark, equal-angle only). TTTG-0167 — asymmetric commensurate p/q TTG BM catalogue (momentum-space, approximate). TTTG-0057 — published N-layer commensurability theorem and the (theta12,theta23,delta12,delta32) deformation space the generator explores. TTTG-0023/0129 — assert no supercell exists for theta12 != theta23; the user refutes this, but it is not a claim of priority.

**Maximally defensible restatement.** Exactly commensurate asymmetric-twist TTG continuum models built by applying a small per-layer biaxial strain, with the layer Dirac points folding onto definite supercell high-symmetry points, are established (Nakatsuji et al. 2023; Yang et al. 2023; Devakul et al. 2023; Xia et al. 2025). Our contribution is not the construction but its exact registration to an atomistic reference: because the continuum model is built on the identical generator-produced supercell and inherits its strain exactly (commensuration residual ~1e-13), model and tight-binding eigenvalues are defined at the same k-points along the same path, enabling an interpolation-free point-by-point band comparison — to our knowledge the first such matched continuum-vs-atomistic comparison for asymmetric TTG — and the certificate is sharp enough to detect an incorrect twist-angle convention and a mispaired tunneling phase assignment.

## A2 - PARTIAL

**Claim.** A2 — Quantitative calibration and transferability: full-path meV-level band-agreement error tables across 9 fitted + 7 held-out configurations (parameters frozen, only an energy offset refit), spanning 2.3–13.2 degrees and two BZ-folding classes, in a literature that otherwise shows only visual band overlays.

**Reasoning.** On the literature question the claim holds. Twenty of the 29 digest entries state explicitly that NO band-structure error metric is reported (TTTG-0166, 0158, 0032, 0194, 0289, 0023, 0129, 0135, 0021, 0095, 0017, 0046, 0065, 0073, 0167, 0163, 0098, 0113, 0203, 0152). The papers that do run their own atomistic benchmark settle for words: TTTG-0043 (Shin/Jung 2026) reports "good qualitative agreement ... small quantitative differences"; TTTG-0133 (Leconte 2022) cross-validates continuum against real-space TB only through magic angles (Table 1, 0.05–0.2 deg tolerance); TTTG-0270 (Morell 2013) diagnoses a sign error in the continuum Dirac shift but gives no RMS; TTTG-0227 gets ~20 meV VHS agreement with STS. No digest paper performs a held-out transferability test with frozen parameters. So the practice gap is real and the claim is not preempted.

What is not defensible is the claim's own arithmetic. Per the audit: TTTG_14_43 appears in both the fitted table and the "nothing was fit to these" table and helped define the frozen medians — six held out, one in-family. It is not parameter-free: a per-config offset up to −29.7 meV is refit on the test data, 8–40× the quoted Chamfer, and those offsets sort cleanly by folding class. The metric is soft (k-blind null already 2–8 meV; band-resolved RMS 2–12× Chamfer; window shrinks with cell size). And no K/K'/K config was ever fitted, so "two folding classes" describes the test set, not the calibration — and that class fails qualitatively (4_45: TB K-gap 23.5 meV vs model 0.41 meV). Reference ceiling: ML-TB is itself 3.7–4.7 meV from DFT, and TTTG-0093 shows such offsets swing tens of meV with TB parametrization.

**Preempting papers.** NONE preempts the error-table/held-out claim. Weak partial limits only: TTTG-0270 (Morell 2013) — continuum benchmarked against atomistic TB on the same commensurate TTG cells, with onsite parameters fitted to TB bands, so "we are the first to benchmark continuum against atomistic TTG bands" is unavailable; TTTG-0133 (Leconte 2022) — quantitative continuum-vs-TB cross-validation table, but on magic angles rather than band energies; TTTG-0043 (Shin/Jung 2026) — independent relaxed-atomistic TB benchmark of the same continuum family, reported qualitatively; TTTG-0238 (Tepliakov 2021) — reports a fit MAE of 0.1–1.4 meV, showing meV-level error reporting is normal in the DFT/Wannier-parametrization literature even if absent in TTG continuum papers; TTTG-0227 (Yin 2014) — trilayer continuum already compared quantitatively to measured spectra at the ~20 meV level. TTTG-0093 (Wu/Zhan/Yuan 2021) does not preempt but undercuts transferability: the Dirac-to-flat-band offset that V2 absorbs swings tens of meV with the underlying TB parametrization.

**Maximally defensible restatement.** To our knowledge no published twisted-trilayer continuum study reports a full-path band-agreement error metric; we report per-configuration mean-nearest-level agreement of 0.8–4.4 meV (0.3–0.8 of the local tight-binding level spacing; band-resolved RMS 1.5–4.6 meV on the small-angle family) for nine fitted configurations and six configurations held out from the fit, with all operator parameters frozen and a single energy offset refit per configuration. The held-out set spans 2.3–13.2 degrees and includes a BZ-folding class absent from the fitting set, on which the frozen parameters fail qualitatively at the supercell-K cone — an extrapolation limit we report rather than a validated transferability.

## A3 - NOT_AN_ADVANTAGE

**Claim.** A3 — "Minimal correction-operator set beyond BM: middle-layer onsite V2 ≈ −50 meV (cross-validated against LCAO pz onsites), particle-hole β|k−K_l|² term, and non-local momentum-dependent interlayer tunneling λ."

**Reasoning.** Each operator is individually published, with numbers. (i) λ: TTTG-0043 uses momentum-dependent tunneling λ_MDT = −2.3 Å, citing prior work, and assigns it exactly the particle-hole role the user gives β; TTTG-0032 uses ξ = −2.1 Å; TTTG-0194 and TTTG-0289 keep the full ĥ(q) non-perturbatively, of which the user's linear λ(d̂·k) is the first-order truncation; TTTG-0133 derives t(G_j) from relaxed hoppings rather than fitting it. (ii) β k²: TTTG-0065 names "k² corrections to the Dirac approximation" as the p-h-breaking term; it is the textbook t′-induced isotropic term in the graphene k·p expansion (Castro Neto RMP), and its bilayer analogue γ4 is used in TTTG-0152, where it flips valley Chern numbers — a sharper payoff than a Chamfer shift. (iii) V2: TTTG-0238 derives layer-resolved pz crystal-field onsites from DFT+Wannier for twisted stacks (MAE 0.1–1.4 meV), i.e. the LCAO cross-check, five years earlier and derived rather than fitted; TTTG-0270 already added onsite ε0, ε1 to a trilayer continuum model fitted to atomistic TB to repair e-h asymmetry, and named vertical tunneling as the culprit λ targets. Middle-vs-outer crystal field is a standard SWMcC trilayer parameter (δ ~ 10–40 meV). "Minimal set" is falsified internally: the ph2 tier fits seven parameters (dw up to 14.6 meV at its bound), and cross-validation is weak — V2 spans −2 to −85 meV across protocols, exceeds the ab initio crystal field (10–30 meV), λ's sign contradicts the two-center form factor, β pins at its bound in 5/9 fits, and the LCAO script is not in the repo.

**Preempting papers.** TTTG-0043 (Shin/Leconte/Jung/Min) — momentum-dependent tunneling λ_MDT = −2.3 Å in a helical-TTG continuum model, introduced explicitly for particle-hole asymmetry; TTTG-0032 (Xia et al.) — same operator, ξ = −2.1 Å, in an exactly-commensurate asymmetric-angle TTG model; TTTG-0194 (Beard/Massatt) and TTTG-0289 (Amorim/Castro) — full non-perturbative momentum-dependent interlayer tunneling, user's λ is its first-order Taylor term; TTTG-0133 (Leconte et al.) — t(G_j) derived from relaxed atomistic hoppings, a stronger version of λ; TTTG-0238 (Tepliakov/Wu/Yazyev) — DFT+Wannier layer- and site-resolved pz crystal-field onsites (incl. twisted stacks, tabulated V0, δ_n, MAE), preempting both V2 and its LCAO cross-validation, and showing the twisted-stack crystal field is ~10–13 meV out-of-plane and dominantly in-plane/moiré-modulated; TTTG-0270 (Morell et al. 2013) — onsite ε0, ε1 added to a trilayer continuum model and fitted to atomistic TB to restore e-h symmetry, with vertical tunneling diagnosed as the cause; TTTG-0065 (Mora/Regnault/Bernevig) — identifies k² Dirac corrections as the p-h-breaking operator; TTTG-0152 (Ma et al.) — γ4 p-h term plus dimer onsite Δ in a trilayer BM model; TTTG-0093 (Wu/Zhan/Yuan) — Dirac-to-flat-band offset swings 18.8 → −23.6 meV with γ0/γ1 choices, undermining transferability of a V2 fitted to one ML-TB parameterization.

**Maximally defensible restatement.** Beyond plain BM we retain three corrections that are individually standard in the moiré-graphene literature — a middle-layer crystal-field onsite (the trilayer SWMcC δ, cf. Tepliakov et al.; Morell et al.), a k² particle-hole term (Mora et al.), and first-order momentum-dependent interlayer tunneling (λ ≈ 2–3 Å, cf. Shin et al.; Xia et al.) — and, for the first time in an asymmetric TTG model, determine them by fitting to atomistic bands: the data require a negative middle-layer onsite of order −50 meV, the same sign and magnitude as the LCAO pz onsite shift, with ±20 meV protocol dependence and a soft (β, λ) degeneracy that we do not resolve.

## A4 - PARTIAL

**Claim.** Scope/coverage: rigid asymmetric commensurate TTG spanning θ₂₃ = 2.3–13.2°, including large angles outside usual BM validity, covering both BZ-folding classes (Γ-class and K/K′/K), with a documented failure-mode and (β,λ) degeneracy analysis.

**Reasoning.** Asymmetric commensurate TTG is thoroughly preempted. TTTG-0167 publishes a full pq-twist catalogue (θ₁₂/θ₂₃ = p/q coprime) with magic-angle tables and a displacement classification the user lacks. TTTG-0163 builds exactly commensurate asymmetric supercells from an integer generator (m,n,p,q) with analytic angles and an explicit BZ-folding figure. TTTG-0098 enforces exact commensuration by per-layer biaxial dilation+twist and already states K₁/K₂/K₃ fold onto Γ, K or M "depending on the exact structure" — that is the folding-class taxonomy. TTTG-0065 (2019) classifies the same geometry by q−p mod 3. TTTG-0032 adds strain-to-commensurate plus registry scanning; TTTG-0194/0289/0203 handle the fully incommensurate complement.

What survives: angle range. Every asymmetric-TTG continuum paper in the digest lives below ~3° (0032: 1.1–1.3°; 0098/0167/0163: near magic; 0203: 0.5–2.0°; 0158 fails below ~2°). None reports any meV band error at any angle. Only TTTG-0270 tests continuum-vs-TB at large angle (1–20°), and it is single-twist and finds the continuum fails qualitatively on the Dirac shift. So "quantitatively benchmarked BM at 9–13.2° in two-angle TTG" is unclaimed.

Two deductions. Folding-class coverage is geometric only: no K/K′/K config entered the fit, and frozen parameters fail there (4_45: TB 23.5 meV vs model 0.41 meV). Documenting failure modes is scientific hygiene, not an advantage; and much of it (the class-correlated offsets, level-count excess) came from the audit, not the docs.

Not covered vs published: relaxation (0066, 0163, 0093, 0133, 0043, 0192 — shifts magic angle ~0.12–0.5°), heterostrain (0021, 0057), displacement field (0023, 0129, 0135, 0152, 0043, 0163), incommensurate/supermoiré (0194, 0289, 0095, 0158, 0203), registry/stacking offset (0032, 0073, 0135, 0167), magic-angle regime, topology, interactions.

**Preempting papers.** TTTG-0167 (Popov–Tarnopolsky): published BM model for asymmetric commensurate pq-TTG, θ₁₂/θ₂₃ = p/q, with full magic-angle catalogue and moiré-displacement classification — kills "asymmetric commensurate TTG continuum model" as novel framing.
TTTG-0163 (Nakatsuji–Kawakami–Koshino): exactly commensurate asymmetric TTG supercells from a four-integer generator with closed-form θ₁₂, θ₂₃, plus explicit BZ folding of layer and moiré BZs onto the supercell BZ — preempts both the generator geometry and the folding bookkeeping, and adds full multiscale relaxation.
TTTG-0098 (Yang–May-Mann–Zhu–Devakul): exact commensuration via per-layer biaxial dilation+twist of the middle layer, generalized BM for θ₁₂ ≠ θ₂₃, and the explicit statement that K₁/K₂/K₃ fold to Γ, K or M depending on structure — direct preemption of the folding-class claim.
TTTG-0065 (Mora–Regnault–Bernevig 2019): unequal twists with rational ratio, p,q coprime, and a q−p mod 3 classification of the Dirac-point geometry — the folding-class idea seven years earlier.
TTTG-0032 (Xia et al. 2025): exactly commensurate asymmetric TTG with layer strain enforcing commensuration, plus registry scanning the user cannot do.
TTTG-0021 (Devakul et al.): per-layer biaxial rescaling κ = cos θ as the commensuration device; K₁,K₂,K₃ collinear and folded to distinct mBZ points.
TTTG-0270 (Morell et al. 2013): TB-vs-continuum trilayer comparison claimed valid 1–20°, i.e. large-angle continuum testing already attempted (single twist, no error metric) — limits the "outside BM validity" novelty to the two-angle, quantitative version.
Partial on the incommensurate complement: TTTG-0194, TTTG-0289, TTTG-0203, TTTG-0095, TTTG-0158 all treat θ₁₂ ≠ θ₂₃ without requiring commensuration.

**Maximally defensible restatement.** The asymmetric commensurate TTG geometry and its BZ-folding taxonomy are established (Mora et al. 2019; Popov–Tarnopolsky 2023; Nakatsuji et al. 2023; Yang et al. 2023); what is new here is the angular reach of a quantitative test — to our knowledge this is the first two-angle TTG continuum model benchmarked against atomistic bands from 2.3° up to 13.2°, well above the sub-3° window in which such models have previously been formulated and never error-tested, and the first to report where it breaks (the K/K′/K folding class, absent from the fitting set, where the frozen parameters miss the TB Dirac gap by ~20 meV) together with the (β,λ) parameter degeneracy. The model is rigid and AA-registry-fixed, and therefore does not address lattice relaxation, heterostrain, displacement field, stacking-offset dependence, incommensurate/supermoiré structure, or the magic-angle regime, all of which are treated in the works cited above.

## Repository audit (independent recomputation)

### What it is

A small, self-contained Python package (~390 lines of model + 8 driver scripts) implementing an exactly-commensurate Bistritzer–MacDonald continuum model for the *generator-built rigid* asymmetric-TTG supercells in `rack:~/ttg_organized`, plus the fitting/comparison harness and the stored results of those fits.

Repo root: `G:/Claude_tmp/Asymmetric_TTG_Continuum_Model`
- Model: `ttg_continuum/rigid_ttg.py` (class `RigidTTG`)
- Drivers: `scripts/compare_rigid.py` (.Band parser + metric), `batch_rigid.py` (plain-BM fit), `fit_ph.py` (λ-less ablation), `fit_ph2.py` (full 7-parameter fit), `predict.py` (frozen-parameter prediction), `fit_onsite3.py` (free per-layer onsites), `compare_dft.py`, `dos.py`, `smoke_test.py`
- Data: `data_rigid/<tag>/openmx.Band` (16 configs), `data_rigid/dft/` (2 configs)
- Results: `results/*_{fit,fit_os,ph,ph2,pred,vfit}.json` + `.npz` eigenvalue archives + overlays

Git history is short and honest: 9 commits, 2026-07-04 → 2026-07-20. It is a research-grade validation study, not a library — no tests beyond `smoke_test.py`, no packaging, no API docs.

Crucially, the object being modelled is a *strained* structure: the generator forces TBG_i (tiled m×m) and TBG_j (tiled n×n) onto a shared hex lattice, so each layer carries a built-in biaxial strain. The model's central move is to put that exact strain into the continuum Hamiltonian rather than approximating it away. This is what makes commensuration exact.

### Implemented capabilities (derived vs fitted)

DERIVED, ZERO FREE PARAMETERS (verified live by me, not just claimed):

1. Geometry from the generator chain. For `TTG_i_j`: f(k)=√(3k²+3k+1), θ(k)=arccos[(3k²+3k+½)/(3k²+3k+1)], a₀=2.47 Å; A_shared=(m·a₀·f(i)+n·a₀·f(j))/2; layer orientations (+θⱼ/2−30°, −θᵢ/2−30°, +θᵢ/2−30°); biaxial scales s₁=A/(n·a₀·f(j)), s₂₃=A/(m·a₀·f(i)). Realized twists θ₁₂=(θᵢ+θⱼ)/2, θ₂₃=θᵢ, θ₁₃=|θᵢ−θⱼ|/2.
   Verified: A_shared(8,25)=109.128163 Å vs POSCAR 109.12816327; (2,7)=32.204721 vs 32.204720585; (14,43)=186.120686 vs 186.12068646.

2. Commensuration certificate 3·B⁻¹·K_l ∈ ℤ². I rebuilt it independently: max deviation 8.4×10⁻¹⁵ (2_7), 1.3×10⁻¹³ (8_25), 8.3×10⁻¹⁴ (14_43), 7.6×10⁻¹⁴ (4_45), 6.3×10⁻¹⁴ (2_47). The certificate genuinely discriminates: substituting the legacy θ₁₂=θⱼ convention gives 3.9×10⁻² (8_25) and 1.3×10⁻¹ (2_7).

3. BZ folding derived, not assumed. Reproduced: 3:1 family (j=3i+1) → [(2,1),(0,0),(0,0)] = L1→K, L2/L3→Γ; TTG_2_12 → K,K,K′; TTG_4_45 / 2_47 / 3_45 / 3_6 → K,K′,K.

4. Integer-arithmetic momentum bookkeeping. Basis = supercell reciprocal points inside (n_shells+½)|b₁ˢ| of each cone; interlayer transfers D_s=(Q_s−Q₀)/3 are exact integer supercell vectors, so supercell Bloch momentum is conserved exactly and model Γ/M/K coincide with the TB path points by construction (`smoke_test.py` checks min|k−K_l| vanishes at the folded cone and nowhere else).

5. Operators available: per-layer Dirac with vfc; BM tunneling T_s=w_aa·I+w_ab·P_s, ph=(0,−2π/3,+2π/3); per-layer onsite V_layer; particle-hole β·|k−K_l|²·𝟙; non-local tunneling t→w(1+λ·d̂_s·(k̃+k)); `pairing="derived"|"legacy"` A/B switch; `mirror` chirality switch. A strain-renormalized velocity hook `vf_strain_beta` exists but defaults to 0 and is unused in every stored fit.

6. Performance: n_shells=12 → 1700 DOF (dim 3400) for every config (the basis is defined in units of |b₁ˢ|, hence scale-free); ~1 s per k-point per valley with shift-invert `eigsh`. Basis convergence verified by me at Γ for 8_25: n_shells 9 vs 12 agree to ≤1×10⁻⁴ meV (n=6 vs 12 differ by 0.049 meV), supporting the "<0.1 µeV at n_shells=9" claim.

FITTED (nothing else is derived):

- Plain-BM tier ("recipe"): nothing fitted except one global energy offset; w=0.11 eV, ħv_F=0.80×6.582 eV·Å taken from literature.
- "fit" tier: (w, vfc, offset) free.
- "ph2" / full tier: **seven** parameters per config — (w, vfc, offset, V₂, β, λ) by Nelder–Mead plus **dw = w_aa − w_ab** fitted in stage A′ and frozen into stage B. The README/DETAILS statement "rigid ⇒ w_aa = w_ab, two free parameters" is contradicted by the stored fits: dw = +14.6 meV at 8_25 (13% AA/AB asymmetry, at the ±15 meV bound), +10.3 at 12_37, +7.3 at 7_22.
- Frozen "tier B" used for prediction: w=0.112, vfc=0.82, V₂=−50 meV, β=−1.9 eV·Å², λ=3.0 Å, **dw dropped to 0**, plus one refit offset per config. So the predictive operator set is not the same as the fitted one.
- Fitting protocol (`fit_ph2.py`): stage A′ matches *sorted* Γ level lists over (V₂, β, λ, dw) at frozen w, vfc with a ±2-state registration scan; stage B refines on the global symmetric Chamfer at stride 8, n_shells 9, 50 eigenvalues; final full-path run at stride 1, n_shells 12, 70 eigenvalues.

CANNOT DO: relaxed structures (rigid only, no corrugation, no AA-shrinking, hence no independent w_AA/w_AB physics); non-biaxial or hetero-strain; strain-induced deformation potential or v_F renormalization; registries other than AA-at-origin; spin/SOC; interactions; anything outside the generator's (i,j,m,n) catalogue.

### Honest limitations and claim-vs-evidence discrepancies

SCOPE
1. Rigid, unrelaxed structures only. No corrugation, no AA-region shrinking; w_AA = w_AB is a structural consequence, not a fit result — and even that is violated in the stored fits (dw up to 14.6 meV).
2. Only the generator's biaxial strain is represented, and only geometrically (rotation + lattice scaling). There is no deformation potential and no strain-renormalized v_F (`vf_strain_beta` is dead code, default 0). Strains are ±0.009–0.044% for the 3:1 fitted family but ±0.12–0.45% for the blind set — the blind configs are 10–50× more strained than most of the training set.
3. AA registry at r=0 is asserted exact; the docstring argues the residual is <0.1 meV but no numerical test of this is stored.
4. Single valley + TRS union; no spin, SOC, interactions, or displacement field beyond a static per-layer onsite.
5. Smallest θ₁₂ reached is 1.52° — the magic-angle regime is not covered.

REFERENCE-DATA CEILING
6. The target is ML-predicted TB, not DFT. On the only two configs with DFT, ML-vs-DFT is itself 3.7–4.7 meV Chamfer (33–35 meV sorted-RMS) — the same order as the continuum model's headline error on the fitted family. Fitting below the ML's own error is not physically meaningful.
7. Both DFT configs are θ₂₃ = 13.17°, explicitly outside formal BM validity, and the comparison uses the plain-BM tier only. There is no DFT check of V₂/β/λ anywhere.
8. E_F is a single dataset-wide constant (−3.6792 eV) for all 16 configs, so a per-config offset refit is legitimate — but it also means the "zero-shot" test is not parameter-free: one offset per config, up to −29.7 meV, refit on the test data itself, i.e. 8–40× the reported Chamfer.

METRIC
9. Symmetric Chamfer has no band identity, no multiplicity check, and is density-biased downward. Null calibration above shows a k-blind model already scores 2–8 meV on the small-angle family. Band-resolved RMS is 2–12× the Chamfer. Reporting "0.8–4.2 meV" without the level-spacing normalization overstates fidelity.
10. The window co-varies with supercell size (fixed 50 TB bands), so the apparent improvement toward small angles is largely a metric artifact.
11. The model has systematically more in-window states than TB at 6_19/7_22/8_25/10_31 and at four of the blind configs — a defect Chamfer cannot see, and one the docs themselves warn about (|β| ≳ 3 pulls remote basis states into the window; the fits sit at β ≈ −2, adjacent to that regime).

FITTING / DEGENERACY
12. (β, λ) soft degeneracy is real and severe: β pins at the −2 bound in 5/9 ph2 fits (stage A′ hits exactly −2.00 in 5/9; λ hits exactly the 6.0 bound at 10_31; dw hits its ±15 meV bound at 8_25). Free 2-parameter (w, vfc) fits slide along the α = w/(ħv·k_θ) valley — 12_37 lands at (0.164, 1.061), 13_40 at (0.148, 0.981), 14_43 at (0.128, 0.601). The docs acknowledge this.
13. Stage A′ requires only ≥4 matched Γ levels while fitting 4 parameters, so it can be exactly determined: 2_7 reports Γ-rms 0.0018 meV and 6_19 ~0 — those are not evidence of fit quality. 2_12's stage A′ never found a feasible point (stored Γ-rms = 10⁶) and fell back to a default start.
14. V₂ is protocol-dependent, not a measured constant: ph2 gives −2.0…−62.9 meV; the λ-less ablation gives −10.5…−84.7 meV; free (V₁,V₂,V₃) with β=λ=0 gives −1.8 meV at 2_12 but −54.9 at 4_45. The quoted "−49 ± 7 meV" is the ph2 subset with 2_7 excluded. The LCAO cross-check (−59 ± 2 meV) lives on `rack:/tmp/pz_onsite.py` and is not reproducible from this repo.
15. λ's fitted sign is opposite to the naive two-center form-factor slope — the docs concede it is an effective absorber, not a derived quantity.

FOLDING-CLASS GAP (the sharpest one, and not stated in the docs)
16. The fitted set contains **no** K/K′/K-class config: eight are (K,Γ,Γ) and 2_12 is (K,K,K′). Four of the six genuinely held-out configs are K/K′/K. So the frozen parameters were never exposed to the class that dominates the blind test — and that class fails: 4_45 TB min|E| at K = 23.5 meV vs model 0.41 meV; 3_45 20.0 vs 11.7; 3_6 25.7 vs 13.6 (2_47 is the exception, 10.66 vs 10.50).
17. The fitted offsets separate perfectly by folding class, not by the "x_4x family" the README claims: Γ-class 11_34 +4.7, 14_43 −10.5, 2_45 −9.1 meV; K/K′/K-class 4_45 −23.7, 3_45 −27.9, 3_6 −28.7, 2_47 −29.7 meV. Since E_F is one shared constant, a class-correlated offset points at a class-specific model deficiency, not a dataset convention. (2_45 is an "x_45" config with only −9.1 meV, which by itself falsifies the "x_4x family" grouping; 3_6 is not x_4x yet carries −28.7 meV.)

TTG_14_43 appears in **both** the fitted table and the "seven zero-shot configs, nothing was fit to these" table, on numerically identical data, and it was one of the configs whose ph2 fit defined the frozen medians. ### TTTG-0166 - Xiao Li, Fengcheng Wu, A. H. MacDonald, "Electronic Structure of Single-Twist Trilayer Graphene", arXiv:1907.12338v1 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] (29 Jul 2019). (Corpus row is labelled TTTG-0165; the PDF filename is off-by-one.)

*File:* `TTTG-0166_Electronic_Structure_of_Single-Twist_Trilayer_Graphene.pdf`

- **Model type:** Pure Bistritzer-MacDonald-type continuum model, solved by plane-wave expansion of four-component envelope spinors in the moire BZ, plus an analytic lowest-order 5-Dirac-state truncation (Eq. 3) used to derive magic-angle conditions. No tight binding, no DFT, no atomistic calculation anywhere in the paper. Extended by the same construction to twisted five-, seven- and nine-layer stacks (Appendix A).
- **Geometry scope:** SINGLE-twist trilayer only: one layer rotated relative to perfect AAA/ABA/ABC. Two families: middle-layer twist (mirror-symmetric, effectively theta12 = -theta23) and top-layer twist (theta12 = theta, theta23 = 0). Both are exactly periodic by construction in the continuum sense, so no commensuration machinery is needed or supplied. Angles 0.5-3.0 deg. Rigid, undistorted layers: no relaxation, no strain, no corrugation, no heterostrain. Fixed interlayer distance implicit; single tunneling amplitude w with no AA/AB distinction.

Momentum boosts q1,2,3 and the sublattice matrices Tm^AB/BA/AA are taken directly from Bistritzer-MacDonald. No fitting to tight-binding, DFT or machine-learned data; no parameter optimization of any kind is described.
- **Validation:** NONE REPORTED. There is no quantitative band-structure error metric anywhere - no meV comparison to DFT, tight binding, or experiment. Validation is entirely internal/qualitative: the plane-wave bands are shown to be consistent with the analytic truncated model, magic angles are reported (1.49 deg, 0.70 deg for mirror TTG; sqrt(2) larger than TBG), a minimum bandwidth of ~20 meV is quoted, and top-layer-twist magic angles are said to agree with earlier work (ref. 16). Numbers such as 7.5e4 m/s at 1.52 deg for ABC are model outputs, not errors.
- **Special terms:** NONE of the user's correction operators. No middle-layer/crystal-field onsite term (the "vertical potential" from the top layer in ABA/top-twist is invoked only as qualitative language explaining a gap, never as a parameter). No particle-hole asymmetry term - the model is exactly p-h symmetric. No nonlocal or momentum-dependent tunneling; tunneling is strictly vertical and momentum-independent. No strain terms. No relaxation-induced terms. Only three q-vectors retained (no higher harmonics).
- **Overlap with this model:** Preempts the general claim "continuum moire model for twisted trilayer graphene, including non-mirror-symmetric stacks": the top-layer-twist case IS an asymmetric configuration (theta12 != theta23, with theta23 = 0) and is fully solved here, as are ABC and AAA variants. It also preempts the layer-projection/mirror-sector analysis. It does NOT do: both angles nonzero, generator-defined commensurate supercells, commensuration certificates, strain, any correction operator beyond BM, or any quantitative band-error benchmarking. With zero reported meV accuracy, it sets no accuracy bar the user model must beat - the user's quantitative fitting-and-validation program is genuinely absent here.

### TTTG-0270 - E. Suarez Morell, M. Pacheco, L. Chico, L. Brey, "Electronic Properties of Twisted Trilayer Graphene", Phys. Rev. B 87, 125414 (2013); arXiv:1301.3052.

*File:* `TTTG-0270_Electronic_properties_of_twisted_trilayer_graphene.pdf`

- **Model type:** Three models used together: (i) atomistic pz tight binding with distance-dependent interlayer hopping; (ii) a continuum model generalizing Lopes dos Santos to the trilayer, diagonalized in a plane-wave basis (Appendix A, H_6x6 Eq. A8 reduced to H_4x4 Eq. A9); (iii) a derived low-energy effective Hamiltonian (Eq. 4) in which two onsite energies are taken as parameters read off from the tight-binding calculation. Explicitly a TB-vs-continuum comparison paper.
- **Geometry scope:** AB-twisted (ABT) trilayer: two Bernal layers plus one OUTER layer rotated - i.e. theta12 = 0, theta23 = theta. Single twist; the two-independent-angle case is never treated. Exactly commensurate supercells built by coincidence-lattice construction, labelled (n,m), N = 6(n^2+mn+m^2) atoms, with cos(theta) = (3i^2+3i+1/2)/(3i^2+3i+1); TB angles 1.35-28 deg, effective Hamiltonian claimed valid 1-20 deg. Rigid geometry: fixed interlayer distance 3.35 A, no relaxation, no strain, no corrugation. Direct hopping between the two outermost layers neglected (checked, shifts values only).
- **Parameter source:** Intralayer gamma0 = -3.16 eV, interlayer gamma1 = -0.39 eV, exponential decay exp[-beta(r_ij - d)] with beta = 3 "chosen to accurately reproduce the dispersion bands calculated within DFT", interlayer cutoff radius 6 a_CC. Continuum tunneling amplitude t-tilde = gamma1/3, and calculations shown for gamma1 = 0.12 gamma0. The two symmetry-breaking onsite terms epsilon0 and epsilon1 in the effective Hamiltonian are NOT derived - they are "considered as parameters given by the TB calculation", i.e. fitted per angle to atomistic bands.
- **Validation:** Continuum is benchmarked against tight binding, and FAILS on part of the physics: the Dirac-cone shift mu0 (and hence the parabolic-band gap) is wrong in both sign and angular dependence. Continuum gives a nearly constant gap gamma1^2/(2 sqrt3 gamma0) for 4-20 deg decaying to zero at small angle, while TB gives a gap growing as angle decreases. Velocity renormalization IS correctly captured. Numbers quoted: TB anticrossing gap 10-40 meV below 1.16 deg; ~90% velocity reduction at 1.35 deg; velocity zero near 0.8 deg. NO global band-structure error metric (no RMS/Chamfer meV number) IS REPORTED.
- **Special terms:** Onsite/crystal-field-type terms epsilon0 and epsilon1 are added to the continuum effective Hamiltonian specifically to restore broken electron-hole symmetry - a direct precedent for the user's middle-layer onsite V2 and particle-hole corrections, fitted to atomistic TB. Crucially, the authors diagnose the ORIGIN: "the tunneling between layers is treated as vertical", neglecting non-vertical hops through odd-numbered carbon rings - exactly the deficiency the user's nonlocal lambda term targets. But they do NOT implement momentum-dependent tunneling; they patch with angle-dependent onsite constants. No strain, no relaxation, no beta|k-K|^2 operator, no higher tunneling harmonics.
- **Overlap with this model:** The most serious partial preemption of the three. Thirteen years earlier this paper already: built exactly commensurate TTG supercells, ran atomistic TB on them, ran a BM/LdS continuum model on the same geometry, identified that plain vertical-tunneling continuum misses electron-hole asymmetry, and repaired it with onsite parameters FITTED TO THE ATOMISTIC BANDS. It also names momentum-independent tunneling as the culprit. It does NOT do: theta12 != theta23 with both nonzero, per-layer strain, machine-learned TB targets, an implemented nonlocal/momentum-dependent tunneling operator, held-out transferability tests, or any quantitative meV accuracy figure. The user's genuinely new content is the two-angle asymmetric geometry with certificate, the implemented lambda operator, strain, and the quantified error budget.

- **Model type:** EXPERIMENT: scanning tunneling microscopy and spectroscopy on HOPG surfaces, with moire patterns created by tilt grain boundaries in the top or second layer. ### TTTG-0158 - Yuncheng Mao, Daniele Guerci, Christophe Mora, "Supermoire low-energy effective theory of twisted trilayer graphene", arXiv:2210.11507v2 [cond-mat.mes-hall], 20 Mar 2023 (Universite Paris Cite / Flatiron Institute).

*File:* `TTTG-0158_Supermoire_low-energy_effective_theory_of_twisted_trilayer_graph.pdf`

- **Model type:** Analytic low-energy (k.p / envelope-function) reduction OF the Bistritzer-MacDonald continuum model for staircase (helical) TTG. BM is taken as the starting Hamiltonian (Eq. 3); local moire Hamiltonians H_M(phi) at fixed supermoire phase are diagonalized numerically, then projected onto n low-energy states to give an effective supermoire Dirac Hamiltonian with space-varying anisotropic velocity plus a non-abelian Berry connection / pseudo-vector potential. Results are DOS maps, velocity minima, and magic-angle maps in the (theta1, theta2) plane. No atomistic or ab initio input at any stage.
- **Geometry scope:** Asymmetric two-angle in principle: valid for arbitrary theta1, theta2 provided theta1/theta2 ~ p/q rational and both angles small.
